About Me

Hi! I'm Carlos Morganti, a passionate artist who creates and shares artwork with the public. I enjoy turning ideas into visual stories and am always interested in learning new styles, techniques, and ways to bring creative projects to life. I have been exploring various forms of art, including graphic design, web design, video editing, and animation. This portfolio is a space to showcase my work and share my creative journey with others.

My family has been a significant source of inspiration and support throughout my artistic journey. They have been encouraging me to pursue my passions and have been there to celebrate my successes and help me overcome difficult challenges I have faced through out this artistic journey. Their belief in my abilities has motivated me to keep pushing the boundaries of my creativity and strive for excellence in everything I do.

I'm using this portfolio to showcase the effort, training, and commitment I've put into my craft, and to see how far I've come. My dream is to work in the entertainment industry, especially in movie and show development. I'm interested in visual effects, green screen, motion tracking, animation, character design, storyboarding, and editing stories together. I hope this work gives a glimpse of what I can do now and what I'm working toward next.
